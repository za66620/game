# Third-party assets

## Kenney Pixel Platformer 1.2

- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/pixel-platformer-0
- Creator: Kenney (https://kenney.nl)
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)
- Local copy: `assets/external/kenney_pixel-platformer/`

The package supplies the terrain tiles, background silhouettes, coins, plants,
robots and flying enemies used by the three redesigned levels. Attribution is
not required by CC0, but is retained here as a courtesy.

The knight sprite sheet in `assets/knight_side_combat.png` was created for this
project from the user-provided knight reference.
